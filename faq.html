Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about orders, shipping, and returns

🔍

How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with a tracking number. You can use this number on our website or the carrier's site to track your package in real-time.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping (2-3 business days) is available at checkout. International orders may take 10-14 business days depending on customs.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for all unworn items with original tags attached. Simply initiate a return through your account or contact our support team. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.

How do I find my correct size?

Check our detailed size guide for measurements. Each product page also includes specific sizing information. When in doubt, we recommend sizing up for a relaxed fit.

Can I modify or cancel my order?

Orders can be modified or cancelled within 2 hours of placement. After that, we begin processing and cannot guarantee changes. Contact customer service immediately for assistance.

Are your materials sustainable?

Yes! We use 100% organic cotton, recycled polyester, and ethically sourced wool. Our packaging is 100% recyclable and plastic-free. Read more about our sustainability commitment on our Our Story page.
